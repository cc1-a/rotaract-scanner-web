🎟️ Pre-Registered Guests

  1. Scan their Ticket: Point your camera at the QR code on their phone or printed ticket.
  2. Wait for Green: Look for the ✅ Green Checkmark and wait for the phone to vibrate.
  3. Verify Meal: Check their screen for Veg 🥗 or Non-Veg 🍗 and hand them the correct food token.

🚶 Walk-In Guests (No Ticket)

  1. Guest Scans You: Show the "Walk-In QR" above and ask the guest to scan it with their phone camera.
  2. Guest Registers: The guest quickly types their Name, Phone, and Club into their phone.
  3. Guest Gets QR: A yellow 'WALK' QR code will instantly pop up on their screen.
  4. You Scan Guest: Point your scanner app at their new QR code to officially check them in!

⚠️ Troubleshooting

  • Already Checked In: If the screen turns yellow ⚠️, the guest has already been scanned. Do not give them a second meal token.
  • Invalid Format: The guest scanned a random QR code instead of the event ticket.
