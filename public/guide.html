🎟️ Pre-Registered Guests

  • Point your scanner at the QR code on their ticket or phone.
  • Wait for the Green Checkmark and vibration.
  • Verify their name and Meal preference on the screen.

🚶 Walk-In Guests

  • Ask them to scan the Gold Walk-in QR Code above using their phone's camera.
  • They will be prompted to enter their Name, Phone, and Club.
  • Once they submit, a dynamic QR code will appear on their screen.
  • Point your scanner at their screen to officially check them in!

⚠️ Troubleshooting

  • Already Checked In: If the screen turns yellow, the guest has already been scanned. Do not give them a second meal/kit.
  • Invalid Format: The guest scanned a random QR code, not the event ticket.
